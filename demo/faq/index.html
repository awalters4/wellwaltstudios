Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

What should I expect at my first appointment?+

Arrive about 10 minutes early. We’ll talk through what’s bothering you and what you want out of the session, then you’ll have privacy to get settled on the table. You’re always in control of pressure and areas worked.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Life happens — reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside 24 hours, a 50% fee applies.

Do you take insurance or HSA/FSA cards?+

We don’t bill insurance directly, but we’re happy to provide a receipt you can submit yourself. HSA and FSA cards are accepted at checkout.

Do you offer gift certificates?+

Yes — any session can be purchased as a gift. Use the contact form and we’ll email a printable certificate the same day.
