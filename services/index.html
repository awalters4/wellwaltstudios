FAQ

Questions owners ask me.

What kinds of businesses do you build for?

Any small business that gets customers online — law firms, clinics and practices, contractors, salons, consultants, local services, shops and restaurants. If a good website and less admin would help you win more work, you're a fit. I don't specialize in a single industry.

Do I really own the site with no monthly fees?

Yes. You own the code, content, and domain outright. There's no platform subscription owed to me. You'll have normal third-party costs like domain registration and hosting, which are yours and typically minimal. The optional care plan is exactly that — optional.

What's included versus an upsell?

The flat price covers the website, intake routing to your inbox, booking, and local-search setup. Deeper automations — document generation, a website chatbot, CRM integrations — are scoped as add-ons when your workflow needs them. We'll decide together on the call.

How long does it take?

Most builds go live within a few weeks. The biggest variable is how quickly content (photos, service details, copy) comes together. We lock a firm date on the call.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, any existing branding or photos, and a bit of time to review previews. If you don't have copy or images ready, I can help with that — it just factors into the timeline.

Can you redesign my existing site or just add automations?

Both. I redesign existing sites and I also add intake automations and workflows to sites that are otherwise fine. Tell me what you need on the intake form and we'll scope it.
