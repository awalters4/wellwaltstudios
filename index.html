Prologue

Hello, I'm Ariel.

I've spent 13+ years building software, the kind that has to work for thousands of people, for companies like Boeing, BAE Systems, Eaze, and Whetstone Education.

Well Walt Studios is where I bring that same care to small businesses. When you hire me, you get me, the person who designs it, builds it, and hands you the keys. No account managers. No passing your project to a junior team.

I'm based in New Orleans and I work with clients anywhere. I like plain talk over jargon, I'd rather you understand exactly what you're getting.

I started young. In 6th grade I figured out how to make pop-up messages appear on my classmates' screens in computer class, and I've been building things ever since. When I'm not working, I'm usually deep in a sci-fi book or show, baking something, or out in the garden.

Chapter 05

Questions people ask me.

How long does it take?

Usually live within a few weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly your words and business details come together.

What does it cost?

The done-for-you website is $699 $500 for the rest of 2026, and you own it. Custom work is priced per project, from $200/hr. You'll always get a fixed price before we start.

Do I need to buy hosting?

I set you up with a free hosting option and a simple how-to guide. It's best if you already own your web address (your domain).

Is there a monthly fee?

No. The first 6 months of help after launch are on me. After that there's no subscription, I help per issue at $125/hr, only when you need me.

Can I edit it myself afterward?

Yes. Your site comes with a simple admin where you sign in and change your details, photos, and pages in plain form fields, then save to publish. I set it up and walk you through it.

What if something breaks?

You email me. During your first 6 months that's covered. After that I fix it per issue, no monthly plan holding you hostage.

Who actually does the work?

Me, Ariel, a senior engineer with 13+ years' experience. No account managers, no junior handoff.
