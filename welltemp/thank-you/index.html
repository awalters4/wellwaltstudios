You're in. Check your email.

A GitHub invite to the WellTemp repo is on its way — usually a few minutes. Here's what to do when it lands.

1

Accept the GitHub invite

Check your email for an invitation from GitHub, or go to github.com/notifications and accept it there.

2

Deploy to Netlify in one click

The repo has a Deploy to Netlify button in the README. Click it — Netlify copies the repo to your GitHub account and puts the site live in about two minutes. No code needed.

3

Log in at /admin and make it yours

Open your-site.netlify.app/admin, sign in with GitHub, and update your business name, colors, services, and contact info. Follow SETUP.md to connect your domain, booking, and payments.
