Next steps

Get your site live in a few minutes

Do these in order. If anything sticks, email us — we answer fast.

  1. Accept your GitHub invite

    GitHub is emailing you an invite to the WellTemp template right now. Open it and click Accept. No GitHub account yet? Create one free at github.com/signup using the same email, then check for the invite.

  2. Make your own copy

    On the template repo, click "Use this template" → "Create a new repository." That gives you your own private copy to keep and edit.

  3. Deploy it — one click

    In your new repo's README, click the "Deploy to Netlify" button and follow the prompts. Your site goes live on a free web address in about a minute.

  4. Make it yours

    Log in at your-site.netlify.app/admin to change your text, photos, services, and links — no code. The full setup guide (payments, booking, connecting your own domain) is in the repo's SETUP file.
