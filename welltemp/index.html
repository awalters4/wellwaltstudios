Questions

The things people ask first.

Is it really no monthly fee?

Right. You pay once for the site. After that your only cost is a domain name (about $12 a year, paid to the registrar — not to us). Hosting, the booking calendar, and the contact form all run on free plans that comfortably cover a normal small-business site.

I'm not technical at all. Can I still do this?

Yes — that's exactly who the Setup Call is for. We get on a screen-share and do the technical parts together, including helping you create a payment or booking account if you don't have one. You leave the call with a working site and a simple login to edit it. If you'd rather not touch any of it, the Full Build hands you a finished site.

How do I take payments?

Your site shows a Buy or Pay button that links to whatever you already use — Stripe, PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App. You paste in your payment link and it works. We'll help you set one up on the call if you need it. (For selling to customers, a business account is worth it — we'll point you the right way.)

Can I change it myself later?

Always. You get a simple admin login where your text and photos are plain boxes — type, drop in an image, save. No code, and no calling a developer every time you change your hours.

What if my site gets a lot of traffic?

The free hosting handles a typical small-business site easily. If you grow into serious traffic, there's a clear upgrade path (a few dollars a month, or a free plan with no limits) and we'll show you how — you'll never get a surprise. You still own everything.

Do I own it, or am I locked in?

You own it outright — the site, the content, the domain. There's no lock-in and nothing we can switch off. That's the whole point.

Refunds?

If we haven't started your Setup Call or Full Build yet, you can get a full refund. Once the work's done and the site is yours, it's final — you have the whole thing. Questions before buying? Email ariel@wellwaltstudios.com and you'll get a straight answer.
