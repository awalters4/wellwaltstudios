Kelova Tempo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 30, 2026

Kelova Tempo (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a shared household calendar published by Well Walt Studios LLC. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive a unique identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email. The app is passwordless — we never collect or store passwords.
  • Household content you create. Events, calendars, members, categories, notes, comments, RSVPs, and reminders, stored to provide and sync the service across your household.
  • Photos you import. When you import a photo or flyer, the image is processed on your device to extract event details. We do not upload or retain the photo.
  • Connected calendars. If you connect Google Calendar, we access the calendars you choose in order to import and sync events. You can disconnect at any time.
  • Location (optional). If you enable travel-time reminders, we use your location to estimate when to leave for an event. It is used in the moment and not stored as history.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and error reports to keep the app stable.

How we use information

To provide and sync the calendar, extract events from photos, send reminders and notifications, process subscriptions, and diagnose crashes. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that operate the service on our behalf: Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions), Google Calendar API (only if you connect it), RevenueCat and the Apple App Store / Google Play (subscription processing), Sentry (crash reporting), and OpenRouteService (travel-time estimates). Each processes data under its own terms. All data is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

Your content is retained while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app, or request deletion at wellwaltstudios.com/kelova/delete-account. Deleting a manager/owner account removes the entire household and all its data; deleting a secondary member’s account removes that member’s data. Deletion is permanent.

Children

Kelova Tempo is intended for household use and is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian manages member profiles; children without their own device are managed profiles with no login.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us at ariel@wellwaltstudios.com and we will respond as required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.
