Delete your Kelova Tempo account & data

You can delete your Kelova Tempo account and its associated data at any time.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kelova Tempo and go to Settings (⚙).
  2. Scroll to Delete Account.
  3. Confirm both prompts.

Your account is deleted immediately. If you are the household manager/owner, this also deletes the entire household and all of its data for every member.

Request deletion without the app

If you can’t access the app, email ariel@wellwaltstudios.com from the address linked to your account (or include the name/household you used) with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify ownership and delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account/profile and sign-in identity
  • Your events and reminders
  • For a manager/owner: the entire household — events, calendars, members, templates, comments, and notifications, plus every member’s profile

What we may retain

We may retain limited records where required by law (for example, transaction records for subscriptions handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play) and anonymized diagnostic data that is not linked to your account. These are not used to re-identify you and are deleted on their normal schedule.
