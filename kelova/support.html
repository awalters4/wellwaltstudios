Kelova Tempo — Support

Need help with Kelova Tempo, the shared household calendar from Well Walt Studios? We’re happy to help.

Contact us

Email ariel@wellwaltstudios.com and we’ll get back to you. Please include your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18) and a short description of what happened.

Common topics

  • Connecting Google Calendar: Settings → Integrations → connect Google, then choose which calendars to sync.
  • Adding household members: Settings → Manage Members. Share an invite code so others can join passwordless.
  • Kelova Plus: Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account, or visit wellwaltstudios.com/kelova/delete-account.
