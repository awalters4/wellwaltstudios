Pricing & ownership

How much does a website cost?

The done-for-you starter is $699 flat — a professional site built on a proven template, set up and launched for you. A custom Full Build, designed from scratch around your brand, starts at $1,200. Prefer to build it yourself? The WellTemp template is $59, one time. Every option is yours to own, with no monthly platform fees.

What's the difference between the template and the Full Build?

The template ($699 done-for-you, or $59 do-it-yourself) is a proven, professional design with your logo, colors, content and photos dropped in — up to five pages, launched fast and affordably. The Full Build (from $1,200) is a custom site designed from scratch around your brand: custom layout, pages written for you, and photos sourced and placed. The step up when the template isn't enough. Compare both on the Services page.

Do I really own the site with no monthly fees?

Yes. You own the code, content, and domain outright. There's no platform subscription owed to me. You'll have normal third-party costs like domain registration and hosting, which are yours and typically minimal.

Is there a care plan or monthly fee?

No monthly fee. Support for the first 6 months after launch is on me. After that there's no subscription — I help on a per-issue basis at $125/hr, only when you actually need me. You handle day-to-day content yourself.

Process & timeline

How long does it take?

Most builds go live within a few weeks. The biggest variable is how quickly content — photos, service details, copy — comes together. We lock a firm date on the intro call.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, any existing branding or photos, and a bit of time to review previews. If you don't have copy or images ready, I can help with that — it just factors into the timeline.

What's included versus an upsell?

The flat price covers the website, intake routing to your inbox, booking, and local-search setup. Deeper automations — document generation, a website chatbot, CRM integrations — are scoped as add-ons when your workflow needs them. We decide together on the call.

Working together

What kinds of businesses do you build for?

Any small business that gets customers online — law firms, clinics and practices, contractors, salons, consultants, local services, shops and restaurants. If a good website and less admin would help you win more work, you're a fit. I don't specialize in a single industry.

Can you redesign my existing site or just add automations?

Both. I redesign existing sites, and I also add intake automations and workflows to sites that are otherwise fine. Tell me what you need on the intake form and we'll scope it.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

Yes. For the done-for-you build I hand you the keys and a walkthrough so you can make edits yourself. The do-it-yourself WellTemp template includes a simple self-serve editor — change text and photos in plain fields and save, no code.

Who actually does the work?

You work directly with me, Ariel — a senior software engineer with 13+ years of experience. No account managers, no handoff to a junior team. I design it, build it, and hand you the keys.
