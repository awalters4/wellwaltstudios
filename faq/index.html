FAQ

Questions owners ask me.

Pricing, process, timelines, and what you actually own. Don't see yours? Ask on a quick call — no pressure.

Pricing & ownership

How much does a website cost?

The done-for-you starter site is $699 flat, built on my proven template. A custom Full Build designed from scratch starts at $1,200. The care plan is an optional $150/mo. You get a fixed price on our call before any work starts.

What's the difference between the starter deal and a Full Build?

The starter deal is my proven template with your logo, colors, photos, and copy dropped in — up to 5 pages, launched fast and affordably. A Full Build is designed from scratch around your brand: custom layout, pages written for you, and sections built to fit how your business works.

Do I really own the site with no monthly fees?

Yes. You own the code, content, and domain outright. There's no platform subscription owed to me. You'll have normal third-party costs like domain registration and hosting, which are yours and typically minimal. The optional care plan is exactly that — optional.

What's included versus an upsell?

The flat price covers the website, intake routing to your inbox, booking, and local-search setup. Deeper automations — document generation, a website chatbot, CRM integrations — are scoped as add-ons when your workflow needs them. We'll decide together on the call.

Process & timeline

How long does it take?

Most builds go live within a few weeks. The biggest variable is how quickly content (photos, service details, copy) comes together. We lock a firm date on the call.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, any existing branding or photos, and a bit of time to review previews. If you don't have copy or images ready, I can help with that — it just factors into the timeline.

Can you redesign my existing site or just add automations?

Both. I redesign existing sites and I also add intake automations and workflows to sites that are otherwise fine. Tell me what you need on the intake form and we'll scope it.

Working together

What kinds of businesses do you build for?

Any small business that gets customers online — law firms, clinics and practices, contractors, salons, consultants, local services, shops and restaurants. If a good website and less admin would help you win more work, you're a fit.

What does the care plan cover?

Hosting, updates, backups and security monitoring; content changes and small additions each month; automation tweaks as your business grows; and priority support when something needs attention. Cancel anytime.

Can I make edits myself after launch?

Yes. Every site comes with training so you can update text, photos, and services on your own. If you'd rather hand that off, that's what the care plan is for.

Who will I actually be working with?

Me — Ariel. No account managers or outsourced teams. You'll talk to the person designing and building your site from the first call to launch.
