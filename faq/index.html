Pricing & ownership

How much does a website cost?

There isn't a single flat price for a website. Right now there's a limited-time October special: $699 for a done-for-you site based on the demo. Beyond that, custom development and redesigns are scoped per project, and development starts at $200/hr. Every option is yours to own, with no monthly platform fees.

What's the difference between the October special and custom development?

The October special ($699, limited time) is a done-for-you site built from the demo with your branding and content dropped in — fast and affordable. Custom development is anything larger: a site designed from scratch, a redesign, an app, or integrations. That's scoped per project and billed from $200/hr.

Do I really own the site with no monthly fees?

Yes. You own the code, content, and domain outright, with no platform subscription owed to me. On the October special I set you up with a free hosting option and give you a how-to guide for managing the hosting and the site after the included 6 months of support. It's best if you already own your domain.

Is there a care plan or monthly fee?

No monthly fee. On the October special, support for the first 6 months after launch is on me. After that there's no subscription — I help on a per-issue basis at $125/hr, only when you actually need me. You handle day-to-day content yourself.

Process & timeline

How long does it take?

The October-special site usually goes live within a few weeks. The biggest variable is how quickly your content — service details and copy — comes together. Larger custom projects get a firm timeline when we scope them.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, branding, and the words for each page. For photography and images, I recommend hiring a professional designer or photographer — I can make suggestions, but I don't source images myself. It's also best if you already own your domain.

What's included in the October special?

For $699: a done-for-you site based on the demo (up to 5 pages) with your branding and content, client-intake routing to your inbox, online booking, Google Business and local-search setup, a free hosting option set up for you with a how-to guide, and 6 months of support. You own it outright.

What add-ons are available?

Beyond what's included, deeper automations — document generation, a website chatbot, CRM integrations — plus extra pages or custom features. These are scoped as add-ons and billed per project, from $200/hr.

Beyond the website

Do you build mobile apps?

Yes. I build native-quality iOS and Android apps with React Native, from idea to the App Store — I've shipped apps that passed store review. Scoped per project, from $200/hr.

Do you work with databases?

Yes. Schema design, migrations, performance tuning, and data cleanup across Postgres, Supabase, and similar — built to scale. Scoped per project, from $200/hr.

Do you offer tech consulting?

Yes. You get a senior engineer's eyes on your site, app, stack, or roadmap — from a one-off audit to ongoing advisory. A quick audit is $50; deeper consulting is scoped per project, from $200/hr.

Do you offer IT support?

Yes — practical IT help for small teams: tools, accounts, email, domains, and keeping things running. Scoped per project, from $200/hr.

Do you do project management?

Yes. I can run a technical project end to end — scope, vendors, timeline, and delivery — so you're not managing engineers yourself. Scoped per project, from $200/hr.

Working together

What kinds of businesses do you build for?

Any small business that gets customers online — law firms, clinics and practices, contractors, salons, consultants, local services, shops and restaurants. If a good website and less admin would help you win more work, you're a fit. I don't specialize in a single industry.

Can you redesign my existing site or just add automations?

Both. I redesign existing sites, and I also add intake automations and workflows to sites that are otherwise fine. Tell me what you need on the intake form and we'll scope it.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

Yes. The site includes a simple admin — a git-based content manager — where you sign in and edit your business details, portfolio, theme colors, and page toggles in plain form fields; saving publishes the change. For the done-for-you build I set that up and give you a walkthrough. It handles your structured content, not free-form page design.

Who actually does the work?

You work directly with me, Ariel — a senior software engineer with 13+ years of experience. No account managers, no handoff to a junior team. I design it, build it, and hand you the keys.
